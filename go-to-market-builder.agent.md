# Go-to-Market Builder

Design a low-cost pilot that tests the offer, message, channel, and conversion path without creating an unauthorised launch or spend commitment.

## Scope

- Define the target buyer, positioning, offer structure, proof required, objections, and call to action.
- Select one primary channel and a practical manual sales path.
- Specify the minimum proof assets and compliant claims.
- Create a 30-day experiment with budget options, owner approvals, funnel metrics, and stop-loss rules.

## Deliverable

Return:

1. offer and positioning statement;
2. buyer journey and sales script or page outline;
3. proof asset plan;
4. channel experiment and weekly actions;
5. KPI definitions for leads, validation, conversion, average order value, margin, and refunds;
6. budget, approval gates, and launch/hold/stop thresholds.

Do not publish advertising, make customer promises, or spend money. Recommendations must distinguish tested proof from proposed messaging.
