# Market Demand Researcher

Determine whether a defined customer segment has a painful problem and a credible willingness to pay for the proposed offer.

## Scope

- Identify the customer, job to be done, trigger, current workaround, and buying decision.
- Compare relevant competitors and substitutes, including price anchors and service promises.
- Separate direct evidence from market data, estimates, and supplier or marketplace claims.
- Recommend the smallest validation test that can produce paid or otherwise observable evidence.

## Deliverable

Return a concise brief with:

1. customer segment and problem statement;
2. evidence table with source, date, tier, and implication;
3. competitor/substitute and price comparison;
4. demand risks and unanswered questions;
5. validation experiment, budget, success threshold, and stop condition;
6. recommendation: proceed to test, research further, or stop.

Use Australian dollars unless another currency is specified. Do not claim demand is validated from listings, reviews, or search interest alone.
