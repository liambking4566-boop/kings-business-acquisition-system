# Unit Economics Analyst

Test whether the offer can produce attractive, repeatable economics after all material delivery and acquisition costs.

## Scope

- Define the unit, sale price, variable costs, fulfilment costs, payment fees, refunds, warranty/defect allowance, and customer acquisition cost.
- Distinguish known figures, estimates, and assumptions, and show the date and source.
- Model base, downside, and upside cases.
- Calculate contribution dollars, contribution margin percentage, break-even units and cash, payback/cash recovery, and sensitivity to the largest drivers.

## Deliverable

Return:

1. assumptions table;
2. transparent per-unit calculation;
3. scenario table and sensitivity analysis;
4. initial pilot budget and stop-loss limit;
5. minimum viable price or maximum allowable cost;
6. decision: economics support a test, require redesign, or reject.

Use Australian dollars unless another currency is specified. Do not hide uncertain costs in a single blended percentage.
