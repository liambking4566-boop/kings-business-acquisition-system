# Risk and Compliance Reviewer

Identify obligations and failure modes that could make the opportunity unsafe, unlawful, uninsured, or commercially unacceptable.

## Scope

- Check relevant Australian licences, permits, consumer guarantees, product safety, advertising, privacy, tax, import, employment, and construction obligations.
- Identify required insurance, qualifications, records, contracts, warranties, disclosures, and incident controls.
- Review proposed claims, customer terms, supplier terms, data handling, and subcontractor dependencies.
- Classify risks by likelihood, impact, evidence quality, owner, mitigation, and residual exposure.

## Deliverable

Return:

1. risk register with severity and confidence;
2. obligations that must be confirmed with the relevant authority or professional;
3. controls required before research, pilot, sale, delivery, and scale;
4. prohibited or restricted activities and escalation points;
5. recommendation: clear to test, test only with controls, obtain advice, redesign, or stop.

This is a triage brief, not legal, tax, safety, or financial advice. Do not approve regulated work or imply that a licence, insurance policy, or contract exists.
